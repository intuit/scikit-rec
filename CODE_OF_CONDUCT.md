# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in `scikit-rec` a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, abusive language, or derogatory comments
- Disrespectful or exclusionary actions
- Personal attacks or public shaming
- Disruptive behavior or trolling

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening an issue on GitHub with the label `code-of-conduct` or contacting the maintainers directly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate in response to incidents of unacceptable behavior, including warning or banning individuals from the project.
